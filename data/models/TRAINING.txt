IRLSAFETY+ — Local-only model training (privacy-first)
========================================================

PRIVACY — READ THIS FIRST
-------------------------
IRLSAFETY+ is built for on-device privacy:

  - Live video never leaves your PC during streaming
  - Windows OCR runs locally (no cloud OCR API)
  - YOLO detection runs locally via ONNX on your GPU/CPU
  - The plugin does NOT upload frames, train in the cloud, or phone home
  - Your PII never trains anyone else's model

Model training (optional) happens ONLY on YOUR machine with YOUR images.
Do NOT upload stream frames to third-party websites (Roboflow, Google, etc.)
if privacy is your goal. Use the local tools below instead.

GOAL
----
A small YOLOv8n ONNX file: irlsafety-detect.onnx
Finds license plates and street signs — tuned for your IRL setup.

STEP 1 — Local training setup (one time)
----------------------------------------
  scripts\setup-training.ps1

Installs ultralytics (YOLO) locally. No cloud account required.

STEP 2 — Collect images (stay local)
------------------------------------
200+ images minimum. Only use footage YOU control:

  - Pause OBS → screenshot your own clips
  - Export frames from your recordings on disk
  - NEVER upload plates, faces, addresses, or IDs to a website

Include variety: angled plates, night, blur, different signs.

STEP 3 — Label images (local tools only)
--------------------------------------
Recommended: LabelImg (free, offline, no account)
  pip install labelImg
  labelImg

  1. Open training\images\train
  2. Set save format to YOLO
  3. Draw boxes — class names MUST match:
       0  license_plate
       1  street_sign
       2  document      (optional)
       3  face          (optional)
  4. Labels save as .txt next to each image

Alternative: CVAT self-hosted on localhost (still your machine).

Put 80% of images in training\images\train, 20% in training\images\val.

STEP 4 — Train locally
----------------------
  cd training
  yolo detect train data=dataset.yaml model=yolov8n.pt epochs=100 imgsz=640 batch=8

All training runs on your GPU/CPU. No data sent anywhere.

STEP 5 — Export ONNX (local)
----------------------------
  yolo export model=runs\detect\train\weights\best.pt format=onnx imgsz=640 simplify=True

  copy runs\detect\train\weights\best.onnx ..\data\models\irlsafety-detect.onnx

Install to OBS:
  copy data\models\irlsafety-detect.onnx ^
    "%ProgramFiles%\obs-studio\data\obs-plugins\irlsafety-plus\models\"

STEP 6 — Test in OBS
--------------------
License Plates + Street Signs ON, Debug Logging ON.
Log should show: Object detection — N region(s)

FUTURE: USER-GUIDED TUNING (planned, still local)
------------------------------------------------
Later versions may let users mark "missed this plate" → save frame locally
→ optional local re-train script. Still no cloud. Still your data on your disk.

MOBILE / LOW POWER
------------------
YOLOv8n stays small (~6 MB). Future phone builds use the same architecture
with smaller input (320px) and on-device quantization — no cloud inference.
