IRLSAFETY+ — Android (planned)
================================

STATUS TODAY (v1.0-alpha P10)
-----------------------------
Android scaffold exists (`android/` in repo) — links libirlsafety via NDK.
Camera preview + live detection ship in P11–P13. Windows OBS plugin remains primary.

USE YOUR PHONE WITH OBS ON A PC (works now)
-------------------------------------------
1. On the phone: Larix Broadcaster, NDI HX Camera, or SRT streaming app
2. On the PC: OBS → add NDI / SRT source
3. Add IRLSAFETY+ filter on that source
4. Stream or record as usual — detection runs on the PC

See PLATFORMS.txt for the full adapter matrix.

ANDROID APP (roadmap)
---------------------
Planned as a standalone camera protection app (not an OBS plugin):

  v0.8  Extract libirlsafety core (shared with Windows) — done
  v1.0  Android alpha — camera preview + plate/mail/ID detection (P10 scaffold shipped)
  v1.1  On-device OCR (same ONNX stack as Windows child OCR)

MVP scope:
  - Rear/front camera live preview
  - Local YOLO detection (irlsafety-detect.onnx)
  - Solid-box censorship
  - 100% on-device — no cloud

Out of scope for first Android release:
  - OBS integration (OBS does not host plugins on Android)
  - Full-screen OCR on other apps
  - On-phone training pipeline

Technical design: docs/DESIGN-android-v1.md in the repo.

PRIVACY
-------
Same as Windows: inference stays on your device. No account required.
