IRLSAFETY+ — Android
=====================

STATUS (0.9.6-dev, pre-1.0)
----------------------------
Standalone camera app in repo (`android/`): preview, ONNX detection, GLES boxes,
settings UI, front camera, 720p/1080p analysis.

USE YOUR PHONE WITH OBS ON A PC (works now)
-------------------------------------------
1. Larix, NDI HX Camera, or SRT on the phone
2. OBS NDI/SRT source on Windows
3. IRLSAFETY+ filter on that source
